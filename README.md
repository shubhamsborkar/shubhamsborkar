## About Me

I am an equity investor. I have been investing in public markets since 2018, and for a few years I managed about $60M of equity on a broking desk. For the last two years I have been working out how AI can take on the heavy part of research, so that one person can do the work a whole desk used to do.

- **Reading filings at scale:** annual reports, 10-Ks, prospectuses and debt schedules, with every figure traced back to the page it came from.
- **Tracking the smart money:** insider Form 4s, fund 13F filings, the options tape, short interest and Congress trades.
- **Building it on my own machine:** research tools that run locally, work with any AI (Claude, ChatGPT or your own API key) and cost nothing to use.

Most of the record an investor needs is public and free, but the tools that make sense of it are either expensive or do not exist yet, so I build my own for my own research, and when something works I open-source it.

## Projects

**[GreekSoup: the one-person equity research desk](https://github.com/shubhamsborkar/greeksoup)**

It runs on your own computer and keeps your book, filings, the options tape, macro, commodities and your research notes in one place, with your own AI plugged in. It is free, open source and updated regularly. More at [greeksoup.ai](https://greeksoup.ai).

Most of the code is written with Claude Code and other AI tools, but the investing frameworks behind it are mine, and I use it on my own research first. Feedback and ideas are always welcome.
