I am an equity investor. I have been investing in public markets since 2018, and for a few years I managed about $60M of equity on a broking desk. For the last two years I have been working out how AI can take on the heavy part of research (reading filings, pulling the numbers, keeping track of what insiders and funds are doing) so that one person can do the work a whole desk used to do.

Most of the record an investor needs is public and free, but the tools that make sense of it are either expensive or do not exist yet, so I build my own for my own research, and when something works I open-source it, because I think a lot of investors are stuck with the same problems I was.

### Projects

| Project | What it does |
|---|---|
| **[GreekSoup](https://github.com/shubhamsborkar/greeksoup)** | The one-person equity research desk. It runs on your own computer and keeps your book, filings, the options tape, macro, commodities and your research notes in one place, with your own AI plugged in. [greeksoup.ai](https://greeksoup.ai) |
| **[options-the-disagreement-detector](https://github.com/shubhamsborkar/options-the-disagreement-detector)** | Checks whether the options market disagrees with your long-term view on a stock you own |
| **[calendar-tell](https://github.com/shubhamsborkar/calendar-tell)** | Reads whether a company is acting confident or scared after its results |
| **[stock-selloff-triage](https://github.com/shubhamsborkar/stock-selloff-triage)** | When a stock falls, works out whether it is the stock, its sector or the whole market |
| **[debt-waterfall-skill](https://github.com/shubhamsborkar/debt-waterfall-skill)** | Pulls a company's whole debt structure out of one filing |
| **[insider-cash-to-equity-screen](https://github.com/shubhamsborkar/insider-cash-to-equity-screen)** | Finds directors and officers who took their pay in stock instead of cash, which ordinary insider feeds miss |

Everything here is free and open source, and each one comes with the method behind it, so you can run it yourself and change it to fit how you invest. Most of the code is written with Claude Code and other AI tools, but the investing frameworks behind it are mine, and I use every one of these on my own research first. Feedback and ideas are always welcome.
